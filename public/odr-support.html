What We Do Not Do. MSME Catalyst does not lend, underwrite, provide legal advice, adjudicate disputes, operate an ODR platform, guarantee recovery, or replace existing schemes, courts, arbitrators, universities or implementation partners. We facilitate structured access to relevant ecosystem pathways. Independent lenders, ODR providers and other institutions retain their own eligibility, commercial, legal and operational decisions.
