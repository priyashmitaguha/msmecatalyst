# Why Dvara should become a founding member of MSME Catalyst

Internal briefing for Dvara and for the MSME Catalyst secretariat. Prepared on 27 November 2026. It is not a page for msmecatalyst.org.

In this note, a founding member is the institution that funds the building of the cluster system. The individual brochure of 5 October 2026 calls that role a founding backer and titles the ask "Founding Support Proposition." The role is the same. The name used here is founding member, because that is the name of the decision. It is not the lender category on the public membership form, and it is not a council seat.

The brochure states no amount. A speaker note, which is a speaker note and not published copy, says no financial return is promised, and that programme funding is sought upfront. This note does not invent a cheque size. It does not promise a return the brochure refuses.

Who inside Dvara has authority to make the gift is not settled. That question is left open on purpose.

---

## 1. The ask

Dvara should become a founding member by funding MSME Catalyst's execution now, while the secretariat, the centres, and the scorecard are still a proposition.

The money builds the four things the brochure lists under "What your backing would build":

1. A small execution team, described as a dedicated secretariat for partner coordination, enterprise follow-through, cluster delivery, and reporting.
2. A focused research programme on successful MSME and export-led segments, their growth drivers, and why earlier interventions succeeded or failed.
3. Four or five anchor-led capability centres, each with a committed anchor, a defined MSME cohort, a clear business problem, and local delivery partners.
4. A shared evidence system: baseline assessments, a capability-centre playbook, and a common outcome scorecard.

The conversation this note recommends is with Dvara Trust. That is a recommendation about the vehicle, not a finding that the trustees have already agreed, and not a finding that the trust deed allows a grant with no return. KGFS should not write the cheque. Dvara Research should not be treated as the donor. Holdings should not be put up as a substitute.

## 2. What a founding member is paying for

MSME Catalyst is the public name of a platform operated by Digital Growth Infrastructure Foundation, a Section 8 company, CIN U94990MH2026NPL468930. The public site calls it "India's neutral convergence layer for MSME growth" and says India does not lack MSME support, it lacks convergence. The brochure's version of the same idea is that support exists and the entrepreneur still has to connect it, and that Catalyst brings the pieces together around a defined cohort, a committed anchor, and a measurable business problem.

The brochure's four pillars are awareness and readiness, faster receivables, capital and market access, and reports, research, and mapping. The public site's four gaps are the cash-flow gap, the market gap, the capability gap, and the growth-capital gap. The growth-capital gap, on the site, is preparation, documentation, and access pathways. It is not a pool of money the platform lends.

The hard limits, which a gift does not buy its way out of, are these.

- The platform does not lend, underwrite, adjudicate, operate an ODR platform, guarantee recovery, or replace schemes, courts, universities, or implementation partners.
- It does not own the lender decision, the legal outcome, the customer relationship, or the participant's data.
- "No participant gets exclusive access or preferred tie-ins."
- Donor funding "does not buy influence over individual lending decisions, ODR referrals, policy positions or case outcomes."
- The closing speaker note says the founding ask supports programme execution and evidence for replication, and that no financial return is promised.

The brochure is a proposition dated 5 October 2026, not a report of centres already running. The PDF describes a three-year programme whose first gate is still "select and establish." The PowerPoint file contains an overlapping later draft of a five-year plan. Speaker notes say the gates remain subject to programme approval. Neither draft is treated here as an approved budget. The public Reports, Blogs, and Events pages are still placeholders, and the About page still says every council seat is "to be announced," even though the brochure names R. Gandhi, Sunil Shrivastava, Pramod Rao, and Ramgopal Subramani, and the letter inviting U. K. Sinha says the first three have joined the advisory board and the governing council.

No paper in the documents folder, and no page on the public site, creates a legal class called founding member, states a fee, or lists a benefit beyond the building of the system. The documents folder had no Dvara file and no articles of association. If the Foundation's articles define founding member as a membership class, that document was not available, and the gift letter should follow it rather than this note.

## 3. Why this is Dvara's gift to make

Dvara's published mission is that every individual and every enterprise has complete access to financial services. KGFS narrows the same sentence to rural India and to financial well-being. Research narrows it again, to suitable financial services and social security for low-income households and small enterprises. The word that matters is suitable. A loan that the household cannot carry, or a loan whose buyer never pays, is not suitability.

Dvara has already said, in public, that this is a systems problem and that systems problems need philanthropic capital before they need commercial capital. On 20 July 2022 Bridgespan quoted Samir Shah, described there as Dvara's executive vice chair. He said that moving the needle on financial inclusion required a systems approach, and that Dvara Trust's method was blended finance: philanthropic capital incubates the institution, and then "philanthropic capital handing over the baton to commercial capital." The examples he gave were Northern Arc and Dvara KGFS.

The baton moved. The commercial credit then met a cycle in which the loan was not the whole problem. CARE's note of 30 March 2026 records Dvara KGFS as on 31 December 2025: assets under management of ₹1,973 crore, down from ₹2,202 crore on 31 March 2025; the joint-liability share of that book at 56 percent, against 68 percent on 31 March 2025 and 83 percent on 31 March 2024; a loss of ₹16.65 crore in the nine months; gross non-performing assets of 7.31 percent; net stressed assets of 37.42 percent of net worth; asset-quality covenants breached on 72.1 percent of debt facilities; and a downgrade to CARE BBB. Enterprise loans were 32 percent of assets under management on 31 March 2025, in both the CARE note of 30 June 2025 and the KGFS section of the FY2024-25 impact report. The chair's letter in that report says livelihood and income-generation loans reached 60 percent of assets under management and does not define the cut. This note does not treat 60 percent as the enterprise-loan share.

That record is not a reason for KGFS to fund a platform. It is the reason a founder of the system should pay for the half of the problem that credit does not solve. The household's cash flow broke. The buyer was not in the branch. The capability and the documents were not in the same timetable as the loan. Catalyst's founding ask is the cost of building that other half once, as a neutral Section 8, instead of asking every lender to fake it inside its own sales process.

Dvara's companies cannot be that neutral place. KGFS has a book. Research has a point of view. Solutions sells plumbing to lenders. A convenor that sits above anchors, buyers, lenders, and capability partners cannot be owned by any one of them without the others reading it as capture. The brochure is offering that convenor before it exists. The founding member is the institution that pays for it to exist without taking it over.

The reason it should be Dvara, and not a donor with no view of the household, is the design window. Four or five centres have not been chosen in any public paper. Scorecard targets, the brochure says, "will be agreed after baseline assessment." A founding member who arrives after those choices funds someone else's definition of a cluster and someone else's definition of readiness. A founding member who arrives now can require that a rural or high-street nano enterprise, a household cash flow, and a failed intervention are still visible when the definitions are written. That is the distinctive thing Dvara has to give. A generic donor can buy a secretariat. Dvara can keep the evidence honest about the household.

This is also the moment Dvara's own theory says the philanthropic money is supposed to be used. The baton has already been handed to the lenders. What has not been built is the shared infrastructure those lenders, and the enterprises, still do not have. Waiting for a commercial case for that infrastructure repeats the original gap. The brochure is explicit that this money does not come back.

## 4. What Dvara should insist on

Four conditions belong in the gift letter. None of them is a right to lend, a right to a customer, or a right to a seat.

1. At least one of the four or five centres is a nano and micro cohort in a rural or high-street market, with a committed anchor if one exists. The condition is the enterprise. It is not the lender. Tamil Nadu is the evidence that this cohort is real in a place Dvara already knows: CARE states Tamil Nadu as 46 percent and, in another sentence of the same 30 March 2026 note, 45.8 percent of KGFS assets under management on 31 December 2025. The public locator, queried on 27 November 2026, listed 161 Tamil Nadu centres out of 366. CARE's branch count for 31 December 2025 is 421 in one paragraph and 424 in another. The locator is not the statutory count. It is enough to show density. It is not a right for KGFS to be that centre's lender.

2. The research programme is free to say that an intervention failed, including an intervention that touches a Dvara company. Dvara Research is the institution that can do that work, if its own board agrees. It publishes in its own name. Customer-level files do not move. Its participation is the work, or the design of the work. It is not the gift.

3. The scorecard does what the brochure already requires, and one thing more. It reports enterprises that improved separately from enterprises reached, and finance sanctioned separately from finance disbursed. The thing more is a field for distress and payment friction that cannot be relabelled as readiness. A scorecard that turns a household's stress into a passing grade is a use of Dvara's money against Dvara's mission.

4. The gift does not buy leads, a preferred-lender clause, a say in any individual credit decision, a fee waiver, or a council seat. The public rule on donor funding is the working rule. The stricter sentence already drafted for the site footer, that the platform does not allocate leads or route enterprises to preferred lenders, should be written into the gift even though it is not yet on the live footer.

Business EMI Protection is described on the brochure as an early-stage concept, not a product. TReDS onboarding is a centre function. Neither is a Dvara product, and neither should be made a condition that Dvara has to build.

## 5. What Dvara gets

What the papers actually offer a funder is that the four things in section 1 get built, that the money is for execution and for evidence that can be replicated, and that no financial return is promised. The governance slide offers reporting on expenditure, delivery, outcomes, and corrective action. Targets come after a baseline, not before.

The inference, and it is an inference, is about timing. The centres and the scorecard can still be shaped. That inference expires once the centres are chosen and the baselines are locked. It is not a voting right, and the empty cards on the public About page do not confer one. The brochure's named advisors and council members are the Foundation's, not the founder's to appoint.

What is not on offer, and should not be negotiated in, is a book of customers, a cheaper liability for KGFS, a co-lending mandate, repayment of the gift, or a guaranteed seat. KGFS may later be one lender in a centre, on the same terms as any other lender. That is not the consideration for the gift. If the gift only makes sense because those enterprises will be sent to KGFS, it should not be made. The platform would have been bought, and the neutrality the money was meant to purchase would be gone.

## 6. Who should not write the cheque

**Dvara KGFS.** On the figures in section 3, the company is repairing a book. A non-deposit-taking NBFC in that condition should not fund a Section 8. The rating note does not describe programme philanthropy as a use of its liabilities. Using it that way would also confirm the reading the gift is designed to avoid: that the lender paid for the cluster.

**Dvara Research Foundation.** Research is a recipient of restricted grants, not a pool that can be retargeted. The Gates Foundation committed $3,895,441 in October 2021 to study how customer protection builds trust among low-income, rural, and women customers, and committed $2,200,000 in October 2026 for customer-protection initiatives in financial inclusion. Those purposes are adjacent to this work. They are not a mandate to endow Catalyst. Research can design and criticise the scorecard. It is not the founding member.

**Dvara Holdings.** Its annual return for the year ended 31 March 2025 does not list KGFS as a subsidiary or an associate. CARE records Holdings at 1.3 percent of KGFS after the May 2025 infusion. Holdings narrates the group. These papers do not show it as the grant-making foundation. It should not be the member of record in KGFS's place, and it should not be assumed to be the donor.

**Northern Arc Capital.** It is not an institution Dvara can commit. On 31 March 2025 promoter holding was nil, and Holdings held 5.14 percent as a public shareholder. The FY2024-25 impact report still narrates Northern Arc inside the group. The share register does not.

**Dvara Trust** is the right conversation. Bridgespan's 2022 account is that the trust is the philanthropic capital. CARE's note of 30 March 2026 calls it the founding promoter of KGFS and records 28.3 percent after the May 2025 infusion. No document opened for this note shows an uncommitted grant line for this programme. The trustees have to decide whether a gift that does not come back is a gift the trust can make. This note recommends that they be asked. It does not say they have said yes.

The brochure is written to an individual. The speaker note calls the closing slide a slide for individual founding backers. An institutional gift needs a letter the brochure does not contain. That is a drafting task. It is not a reason to route the money through KGFS, and it is not a reason to pick a person inside Dvara and treat them as the donor. No such person is proposed here.

## 7. The decision inside the yes

Dvara's published model hands philanthropic capital to a commercial company and then expects that company to earn a return. Catalyst's founding slide does the opposite. The money does not come back, and the platform does not become an asset Dvara can sell, consolidate, or point at its own customers.

A yes is therefore not another company for the venture studio. It is a grant to a Section 8 that holds the infrastructure for the whole ecosystem. If the only capital Dvara can release is capital that must come back, the honest answer is not to become a founding member, and not to disguise the gift as a KGFS expense or a Solutions contract. If Dvara can still make a systems grant that does not come back, this is the grant that matches the hole in its own system. The commercial half has been built. The shared half has not. The definitions of a centre and of a scorecard are still open. The household is still at risk of being designed out of them.

## 8. Recommendation

Ask Dvara Trust to become the founding member, funding the four uses in the brochure, with the four conditions in section 4 written into the gift. Do not name an amount. Do not request a council seat. Do not file a KGFS lender application as a substitute for the gift, and do not fund the gift from KGFS.

Directors of the Foundation can receive the approach. Prashant Mahagaonkar and Govind Dichwalkar sign the Foundation's letters. The published Partnerships and Membership role is still "Appointment in progress." The approach does not have to wait for that card. It does have to wait for a Dvara decision this note cannot make, which is who has authority to bind the gift.

If that authority cannot be found, or if the only available money is money that must earn a return, do not proceed. A logo, or a lender membership dressed up as founding membership, would spend the only distinctive thing Dvara has to offer this platform: payment for a system it does not control, made early enough that the household is still in the design.

## 9. Open questions

- Who inside Dvara is authorised to make this gift? The answer was left open. This note recommends the trustees of Dvara Trust and does not know whether the trust deed agrees.
- Does the trust deed allow a grant to a Section 8 with no financial return and no residual interest in the platform?
- What amount would be enough to matter at the first gate? Nothing in the brochure, the agreements, or the public site states a figure. A figure should come from the Foundation's budget, not from this note.
- Can the gift letter be institutional, given that the brochure speaks to an individual founding backer?
- Do the Foundation's articles define founding member as a legal class? They were not in the documents folder.
- Will Research's board accept a scorecard role that is allowed to criticise a Dvara company, without being read as that company's research department?
- The chair's "60 percent of assets under management" still has no definition and no balance-sheet date. It is not needed to make this gift, and it should not be cited as the enterprise-loan share.
- Which NBFC was already live on KGFS's partnership book as of 30 March 2026? If that partner is also being asked for founding money, the neutrality design changes. The partner is not named in the CARE note.
