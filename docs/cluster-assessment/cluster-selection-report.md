# Cluster fill-in score

The working instrument is `Cluster-Fill-Score.xlsx` at the repository root. Open the **Scores** sheet. Type a fact, then a score from 1 to 5. The mark out of 100 recalculates. A blank score is dropped. It is not counted as zero.

A tier (Open, Shortlist, Watch, Not now) appears only after 8 of the 12 factors are filled. Until then the tier says **Not yet** and the row is a running score, not a decision.

The same pattern is on the canvas `canvases/cluster-fill-score.canvas.tsx`. What you type there is kept. Weights are editable. Changing a weight rescores every cluster.

## The twelve factors

| Factor | Starting weight | What you write |
|---|---|---|
| How many exist | 12 | Enterprises in the belt you would staff, and the source |
| Growth potential | 10 | What is rising, with a comparison |
| Partners to deliver | 12 | Who would deliver here. A ministry is not a partner |
| Nearby colleges | 6 | College, department, person |
| Lending companies | 10 | The branch or NBFC that will take files. CGTMSE is not a lender name |
| Marketing companies | 6 | A firm that will put units in front of buyers |
| Export potential | 10 | Export of the MSME in this belt |
| Chance of a big listing | 5 | A unit that could reach an SME IPO or the main board |
| Size mix and max lot | 10 | Nano, micro, small, medium, or large — and the biggest lot you would enrol |
| State government support | 8 | Scheme, park, or nodal office, and the state |
| Central government support | 8 | A scheme an MSME here can file |
| Anything else | 3 | One fact that would change the decision. Raise the weight if it should count more |

Weights are relative. They do not have to sum to 100. The mark is the weighted average of the scores you have entered, divided by 5, times 100.

Score 1 is weak. Score 3 is usable. Score 5 is ready. The anchors are on the **How to score** sheet.

## What is already filled

Desk starting scores, in blue, are only where a published figure maps onto the anchor: how many exist, size mix, export, and government support. Partners, colleges, lenders, marketing companies, and listing are yellow and empty. Nano means own-account or a self-help group. Micro is turnover at or below ₹10 crore. Small is ₹10–100 crore. Medium is ₹100–500 crore. Large, above ₹500 crore, is not the lot a centre enrols.

There are 31 rows and 15 blank rows. If one industry has two places, copy the row. Ambur and Agra are not one score.
